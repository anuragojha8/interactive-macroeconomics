Chapter 3 · the steady state

Where building and wearing out cancel

chart table
Investment against depreciation Both are flows, in $/year. Capital rests where they cross.
s̄Y — built
d̄K — worn out

Left of the crossing, more is built than wears out and capital grows. Right of it, the reverse. Diminishing returns bend the blue curve over; depreciation does not bend. They can only meet once.

Capital stock, 120 years from K₀ = 1,000 Fast at first, then barely moving. Half the gap closes in ten years.

By year 120 capital is within 0.02% of the closed form, and growth is down to 0.0005% a year. Left alone it reaches exactly zero — and stays there forever, unless productivity moves.
